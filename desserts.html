Chocolate Chip Cookies

Soft, warm, and delicious chocolate chip cookies that are perfect for any occasion.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup butter, softened
  • 1 cup white sugar
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 2 cups chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F.
  2. Cream together the butter and sugars.
  3. Add the eggs and vanilla.
  4. Mix in the flour and baking soda.
  5. Stir in the chocolate chips.
  6. Place spoonfuls of dough onto a baking sheet.
  7. Bake for 10–12 minutes.
  8. Allow the cookies to cool before serving.
